Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q: How do I fix "No Internet Connection / Network Error" in CapCut PC?

This common issue occurs in certain regional ISP routing environments. To fix it, ensure your Windows clock is synced automatically (Settings ➜ Time & Language ➜ Sync Now), or use a standard secure VPN connected to the US, Singapore, or Germany to allow CapCut’s template servers to load.

Q: How do I fix video playback lag and preview stuttering?

In CapCut PC, go to Settings ➜ Performance and check "Speed up hardware decoding" and "Speed up hardware encoding". Additionally, enable Proxy Mode (720p) under Project Settings for buttery smooth 60fps editing.

Q: Is this CapCut setup completely free?

Yes! CapCut Desktop is 100% free to download and use without mandatory subscription watermarks. All basic and advanced editing tools (transitions, speed curves, text styles) are fully unlocked in the free version.
