Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | CoinRush Hub (mycoinrush.online)

At CoinRush Hub (accessible from mycoinrush.online), the privacy of our visitors is of supreme importance. This document details the types of information recorded and how we handle user data.

1. Information We Collect

CoinRush Hub is a public link directory. We do not require account registration or login to view or click links:

  • Standard Log Data: Like most web servers, we record standard non-identifiable log data such as browser type, referring URL (e.g. Instagram in-app browser), date/time stamps, and ISP.
  • Local Preferences: When you add custom links using our built-in link manager, your preferences are saved locally on your device via HTML5 localStorage. We do not transmit or sell this data.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

CoinRush Hub displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our website based on prior visits to our site or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant advertisements to users.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party advertising cookies by visiting AboutAds.info.

3. Outbound External Links

Our directory contains links to external mobile applications (Google Play, Apple App Store) and third-party websites. When you click an external link, you leave CoinRush Hub. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms.

4. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

We fully respect user rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because we do not store personal profiles or sell consumer data, your privacy remains completely protected.

5. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data inquiries, please email us at support@mycoinrush.online or visit our Contact Page.
