💡 Complete Guide: How to Generate & Send Real Money to Users as Host

When users convert virtual coins into real money on CoinRush (1,000 Coins = ₹10 INR), the coins are deducted instantly from their balance. As the host, you hold the payments queue and send the money via UPI or Bank Transfer within 24 hours.

Step 1: Check Pending Queue Open the "⏳ Pending Payout Queue" tab to see which players have requested withdrawals. Each entry shows the exact ₹ amount and player name.
Step 2: Copy Details in 1-Click Click "📋 Copy UPI" or "📋 Copy A/C" & "📋 Copy IFSC". The details are instantly copied to your clipboard without any manual typing mistakes.
Step 3: Send Money via PhonePe / GPay / NetBanking Open PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or your bank app. Paste the UPI ID or Account Number + IFSC, enter the ₹ amount, and complete the transfer.
Step 4: Mark Paid with 12-digit UTR After transferring, copy the 12-digit UPI Reference / UTR Number from PhonePe/GPay. Click "✅ Mark Paid" in this panel, paste the UTR, and click Confirm. The player's website status changes to Completed!
⚠️ Fraud Prevention & Reject Option: If a player provided an invalid UPI ID or suspicious activity, click "❌ Reject". Their coins will be safely refunded back to their website account automatically, and a rejection reason will be displayed to them.
